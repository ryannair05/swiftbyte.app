FAQ

Questions

Is Halls free? +

Yes. Menus, CATA, Discover and BTChat are free. There’s an optional Pro subscription for plate building, nutrition history and a few extras. Current pricing is in the app.

Which schools does it work for? +

Dining menus work for Penn State University Park, Barnard and Columbia, and the University of Georgia. CATA, PSU Eats, Discover and the rec schedules are Penn State only.

Is there an Android version? +

Yes, Halls is on Google Play. The screenshots here are from the iPhone app, so a few things look different on Android.

Where do the menus and nutrition info come from? +

From what each school’s dining services publish. Menus, hours and ingredients can change, so if you have an allergy, double-check with the dining staff.

Do I need an account? +

No. There’s nothing to sign up for.
