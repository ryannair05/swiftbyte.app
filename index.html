A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
head out.

Is Halls free? +

Yes, the app is free to download. Explore dining menus, CATA and campus features, with an optional Pro subscription for plate building, nutrition history and extra customization. See current pricing in the app.

Which campuses are supported? +

Dining menus are supported at Penn State University Park, Barnard College and the University of Georgia. CATA, PSU Eats, Campus Rec and My Meals are part of the Penn State experience.

Can I use it on Android? +

Yes. Halls is also on Google Play. This page showcases the iOS app. Features and appearance can differ between platforms.

Where do menus and nutrition come from? +

The app organizes information published by university dining services. Menus, hours, ingredients and nutrition can change. For dietary or allergy questions, confirm details directly with dining staff.

Do I need an account? +

You can browse dining menus and CATA without creating a Meet profile. The Meet social feature uses an account; BTChat connects with nearby users over Bluetooth.
