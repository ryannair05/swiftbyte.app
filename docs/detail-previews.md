# Detail-page still images

Each dining hall and CATA uses its own WebP still from the Rotato-rendered source footage. Published images are in `assets/details/`; unused source clips and intermediate renders have been removed from the repository. No videos or playback scripts are shipped.

The five dining hall images use distinct static CSS orientations (rotation and perspective). Each keeps its complete device visible.

Official dining-area names, verified September 14, 2026 against https://liveon.psu.edu/university-park/dining :

- North Food District
- East Food District
- South Food District
- West Food District
- Pollock Dining Commons

East uses `east.webp`, optimized from the supplied `east.png` screenshot at 660 × 1434. Its device bezel is CSS; other hall images retain their Rotato-rendered frames. The original PNG and superseded frame were removed after conversion.
