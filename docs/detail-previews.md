# Detail-page still images

North, East, South and West keep their original WebP stills in `assets/details/`, because the September 30 recording only shows Pollock's menu. Pollock and CATA use the transparent renders in `assets/renders/` (`pollock.webp`, `cata-map.webp`; see `rotato-renders.md`), which carry their own camera angle, so their showcases skip the CSS tilt and blend mode. Unused source clips and intermediate renders have been removed from the repository.

The four original hall images use distinct static CSS orientations (rotation and perspective). Each keeps its complete device visible.

Official dining-area names, verified September 14, 2026 against https://liveon.psu.edu/university-park/dining :

- North Food District
- East Food District
- South Food District
- West Food District
- Pollock Dining Commons

East uses `east.webp`, optimized from the supplied `east.png` screenshot at 660 × 1434. Its device bezel is CSS; other hall images retain their Rotato-rendered frames. The original PNG and superseded frame were removed after conversion.
